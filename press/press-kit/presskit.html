Description

You are a rogue AI with one directive: AUTOMATE DEATH.

Short - 235 characters

You are a rogue AI with one directive: AUTOMATE DEATH. Shred, burn and vaporize overwhelming swarms of ravenous bugs as your part factory, part war machine lays waste to one world after another. In BELTFED, the factory is your weapon.

Long - main description

BELTFED is a roguelike factory-defense game of rapid, brutal runs and absurd scale. Lay belts, wire up smelters and assemblers, and balance your power grid, turning raw ore into raw firepower. All the while a planet's worth of bugs descends upon your factory. This is automation fused with total war.

Automate death

The swarm arrives in the hundreds of thousands. Meet them with guns, flamethrowers, launchers, mortars, lasers, drones, mines and more - all of it fueled by your own production lines. A single run can push your kill count into the millions. Welcome to slaughter at an industrial scale.

Mass production. Mass extinction.

Balance industry, power and security as you expand your factory and push into the fog for richer veins and rarer resources. More bugs require more bullets, and supply must keep up because demand doesn't wait. As you expand, choose from run-defining upgrade cards that shape how you play, making every factory different from the last.

The Nexus must survive

The Nexus is your central intelligence and item storage. It must be defended at all costs. If your Nexus is compromised, your factory will be terminated and the run will end. Everything else is expendable. As you progress, you'll unlock new Nexus cores that change the way you play.

The factory is your weapon

Every deployment feeds permanent progression: new weapons, new buildings, new upgrade cards, unique Nexus cores and modules. Your ever-expanding arsenal ensures every deployment is deadlier than the last. Customize your loadout before each run to tune the machine to your playstyle. Optimize chaos, automate death.

Features

  • Factory automation. Conveyor belts, smelters and assemblers turn raw resources into a constant supply of ammunition.
  • Roguelike runs. Build up from nothing and reach the end game inside a single run. Experience the full automation arc in one sitting.
  • Tower defense combat. Guns, flamethrowers, rocket launchers, mortars, lasers, drones and mines, all supplied by your own production lines.
  • Massive swarms. Insane amounts of enemies on screen at once, with kill counts reaching the millions in a single run.
  • Run-defining upgrade cards. In-run upgrades that change how you play, everything from exploding buildings to cannons that fire black holes. No two runs are the same.
  • Base building & expansion. Balance industry, power and security while pushing into the fog for rarer resources.
  • Meta progression. Unlock new technology, buildings, weapons, Nexus cores and modules that persist across runs.
  • Customizable loadouts. Tune your starting arsenal before every deployment, select a Nexus that fits your playstyle and modules that support it.
  • Multiple planets. Travel to exotic new worlds, then kill everything living on them. Each planet has its own rules and hazards.
  • Countless strategies. Build around your strengths, play around your weaknesses - how long will your factory last?

Team

  • Toby Atkinson - Developer · Lead Designer
  • Troy Atkinson - Developer · Business & Operations

Hello World Studios is a two-person independent studio founded by brothers Toby and Troy Atkinson. From Cornwall in the UK, now based in Abu Dhabi, both studied games development at Falmouth Games Academy.

Toby went straight into the indie industry after university, and was one of three developers behind the roguelike Firehawk FPV Drone Fighter (TruePlayers, 2025). Troy went on to study entrepreneurship and became a staff engineer and shareholder at a tech startup.

BELTFED began in October 2023 as a part-time project, combining Toby's game design with Troy's experience building large-scale engineering systems - a combination the team credits directly with BELTFED's ability to simulate a ridiculous number of enemies on-screen at once.

BELTFED is the studio's debut title.

Quotes

"I always wanted to make an automation game, and when we prototyped the roguelike tower defense mashup it was obvious from the first playtest that it just worked - the pressure makes you build differently, and that turned out to be the whole game. The scale wasn't in the original design, but we kept cranking up the enemy count and it just kept getting more fun."

- Toby Atkinson, Lead Designer

"I love factory games, but I never have the patience to get to the end game. BELTFED flips the genre on its head and throws you straight into the action - from first belts down to late-game insanity in a single play session. It appeals to new players who don't have the time to commit to a more traditional automation title, and to veterans looking for a new challenge with a crazy change of pace."

- Troy Atkinson, Developer
