Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15/07/2026

General

Aside from our mailing list (described below), we don't collect personal information about you, and we never sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

Mailing List

On beltfedgame.com you can join our mailing list to receive occasional updates about BELTFED and Hello World Studios, signing up is entirely optional.

When you subscribe, we collect the email address you provide. We use this information only to send you the updates you signed up for. We do not sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties for their own marketing.

We use Kit (formerly ConvertKit) to manage our mailing list and send emails on our behalf. Your details are stored and processed by Kit in line with their Privacy Policy.

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of every email we send, and you may ask us to access, correct, or delete your information by contacting us at hello@helloworldstudios.io. We'll remove you from the list promptly.

Third-Party Services

Our website and games may link to or run on third-party services, such as social media platforms, operating systems (e.g. Windows), and distribution platforms (e.g. Steam). These services may collect your personal information and are governed by their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review. We are not responsible for the practices of third-party services.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us at hello@helloworldstudios.io.
